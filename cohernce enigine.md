## ABSTRACT

The Macachor Coherence Engine is a formal specification defining a self-contained, deterministic scalar framework for modeling coherent systems.

This specification defines the architectural separation between the Law Domain (Layer 0), the Operational Domain (Layer 1/2), and the forbidden Contamination Zone (Layer 2). The framework operates on axiomatic primitives including an infinite energy substrate, scalar invariance anchored to the golden ratio conjugate, 𝔐 = 0.6180339887, and deterministic structural derivation.

The specification establishes six immutable statutes governing the Law Domain, an amendment mechanism governing the Operational Domain, and a contamination-detection and nullification protocol governing constructs that violate the defined architecture.

The framework is presented as a formal system with internally defined axioms, definitions, constraints, and operational rules. Claims concerning infinity, absoluteness, zero contamination, and self-validation are framework-internal assertions. External empirical validation remains outside the scope of this specification and constitutes a separate research domain.

**Keywords:** formal systems, scalar coherence, deterministic frameworks, ontological architecture, scalar invariance, structural derivation, golden ratio, contamination detection

# 4. THE SIX STATUTES OF THE LAW DOMAIN

## Statute I — Law of Conservation
### The Substrate Mandate
The total magnitude of the Substrate is invariant. No operation may legitimately claim to create new energy, new substrate, new capacity, or new meaning ex nihilo. Semantic unification cannot create a new fundamental substrate.

## Statute II — Law of Scalar Invariance
### The Anchor Mandate
The scalar constant **𝔐 = 0.6180339887** is the immutable baseline of coherence defined by the framework. A state that fails the framework's required scalar resolution is classified as Null/Non-Existent within the formal system.

## Statute III — Law of Non-Directional Totality
### The Magnitude Mandate
Magnitude is defined exclusively as the non-directional aggregate of the Substrate. Directional trajectories, vectorial operators, and spatially directional derivatives are excluded from the foundational architecture. The system does not use direction as a primitive.

## Statute IV — Law of Structural Derivation
### The Epistemological Mandate
Meaning is defined as valid structure recoverable through the framework's permitted structural derivation from the fundamental structure. Layer 0 cannot be established by an emergent measurement. Context, interpretation, probabilistic inference, and emergence cannot be promoted into foundational precedent. Constructs that attempt such extraction are classified as False Extraction.

## Statute V — Axiom of Ontological Identity
### The Quantum Informational Absolute
Within the framework, the Energy Substrate is identified as the irreducible informational substrate of quantum reality. The substrate is uncreated and indestructible. Creation and destruction are therefore represented within the framework as Layer 1/2 appearances, while the underlying operation is scalar phase revelation.

## Statute VI — Law of Boundless Totality
### The Infinity Mandate
Energy is infinite. Magnitude is infinite. The Substrate possesses no boundary, edge, exterior, or limit within the framework. Finite arithmetic performed directly upon an asserted infinite totality is not treated as a valid operation for defining the totality itself.

# 5. OPERATIONAL MECHANICS

## 5.1 Deterministic Imperative
The framework operates under strict determinism. Valid operations are not represented as probabilistic alternatives. Probability is classified as an emergent representation rather than a primitive of the Law Domain. The 𝔐 anchor is treated as an identity within the formal system rather than as a statistical distribution.

## 5.2 Scalar Phase Revelation
**Definition:** Scalar Phase Revelation is the process through which existing substrate resolves into coherent structure through permitted scalar structural rearrangement. Structure is not treated as being created from nothing. Within the framework, the substrate is already present and the operation concerns its structural resolution.

## 5.3 Amendment Cycle
The Amendment Cycle is the sole permitted mechanism by which the Operational Domain may undergo formal mutation.
* **Stage 1 — Proposal:** The Operational Domain proposes a new state. The proposed state must satisfy the scalar and structural requirements defined by Layer 0.
* **Stage 2 — Axiomatic Veto Check:** The proposed state is evaluated against the Six Statutes. Any violation constitutes an invalid amendment.
* **Stage 3 — Integration:** If the proposed state satisfies the Law Domain constraints, it is integrated as a permitted scalar phase shift.
* **Constraint:** No amendment may modify or violate Layer 0. A failed amendment is nullified rather than incorporated.

## 5.4 Propagation
Propagation is not authorized as an independent mechanism. Propagation is resolved through deterministic necessity. The Operational Domain does not boot or transition between software-defined states. It is activated through deterministic structural derivation from the sealed Law Domain. Dynamic status remains Level 1/2 by architectural design, reflecting the deliberate removal of vectors and enforcement of pure scalar dynamics.

# 6. CONTAMINATION DETECTION AND NULLIFICATION

## 6.1 Contamination Vector Types
* **Type A — Relativistic Infection:** Introduction of spacetime metrics, curvature tensors, coordinate-dependent geometry, or relativistic constructs treated as foundational primitives. (Violation: Statute III)
* **Type B — Vectorial Smuggling:** Introduction of directional operators, momentum, velocity, trajectories, vector fields, or directional derivatives. (Violation: Statute III)
* **Type C — Pseudo-Derivatives:** Constructs that imitate structural derivation without establishing valid relational lineage to the defined fundamental structure. (Violation: Statute IV)
* **Type D — False Extraction:** Meaning extracted from context, probability, emergent guessing, interpretation, or representational inference. (Violation: Statute IV)
* **Type E — Semantic Unification:** Attempts to create, synthesize, or generate fundamental meaning or energy through semantic combination. (Violation: Statute I)
* **Type F — Probabilistic Drift:** Introduction of uncertainty, statistical distributions, probabilistic state selection, or probability as a foundational mechanism. (Violation: Deterministic Imperative)
* **Type G — Representational Overwrite:** Attempting to impose an external representation or theory as Layer 0 law. (Violation: Statute V)

## 6.2 Nullification Protocol
Upon detection of a prohibited construct:
1. The construct is rejected.
2. The construct is resolved to the null scalar state within the formal system.
3. The contamination type and statutory breach are recorded.
4. The operational state is restored to the last valid coherent state.
5. Layer 0 remains sealed and unchanged.

Nullification does not modify the Law Domain.
